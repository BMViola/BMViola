🌊 I'm a Biological Oceanographer, currently working as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow (Quantitative Ecology) at [Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/science/schools/biological-sciences), where I work with the [Research Ecology Conservation Group](https://www.researchecology.com/) to **quantify seabird community dynamics using biotelemetry** 📡. I completed my PhD at the [Institute for Marine and Antarctic Studies](https://www.imas.utas.edu.au/), University of Tasmania (graduated August, 2026). My thesis focussed on the marine habitat use of Snow Petrels (*Pagodroma nivea*) in East Antarctica and the Southern Ocean 🧊, with an emphasis on improving at-sea data collection methods.

🚁 I have led and contributed to **field programs in remote environments**, as a Seabird Ecologist and Lead Vertebrate Biologist at Mawson and Davis Stations, Antarctica, and then as a Wildlife Biologist at Heard Island with the Australian Antarctic Division ❄️. I have also worked as a Senior Wildlife Biologist on offshore and onshore windfarms 🌬️, and as a JNCC qualified marine mammal observer 🐳 on anchor handling vessels ⚓, mobile offshore drilling units 🛢️ and seismic survey vessels 🚢.

🛰️ Alongside my research, I endeavour to **build tools and refine methods that make environmental data easier to acquire and use in the ecological space**. Most recently, I wrote [Copernicopia](https://github.com/BMViola/copernicopia), an R package that walks you through downloading [Copernicus Marine](https://marine.copernicus.eu/) data and appending it to your own locations and times.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- Marine vertebrate ecology, from individual movement to population and community dynamics
- Biological oceanography, particularly bio-physical coupling: how upwelling, frontal systems and sea ice shape the distribution of marine predators
- Wildlife health, including impacts of highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI H5N1) and contaminants such as plastics and per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS)
- Marine habitat use and at-sea distribution from biotelemetry and habitat modelling
- Ecological survey design and at-sea data collection methods
- Integrating satellite remote sensing and ocean model reanalyses with animal tracking data
- Building evidence bases that serve conservation management objectives for threatened species
- Understanding how climate change is altering marine ecosystems and the species that depend on them
